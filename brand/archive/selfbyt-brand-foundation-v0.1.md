# Selfbyt: Brand foundation v0.1

Working proposal | 2 October 2026

Based on existing project materials and founder direction. Visual choices are proposals; project capabilities have not been independently benchmarked.

## Foundations for intelligence.

Selfbyt is an experimental AI company building infrastructure for efficient intelligence, with model development as a future direction.

### The central question

How much memory and computation does useful intelligence really need?

### The mission

Experiment with how AI is represented and executed, then turn useful findings into infrastructure people can build on.

### The ambition

Build a path from more efficient AI systems to models designed with those systems in mind.

### The character

Curious in the questions. Precise in the work. Direct about the results. Ambitious about what comes next.

Proposed lead line: Foundations for intelligence.

## A direction already taking shape.

The strongest common thread in the work is the relationship between model structure, execution cost, and useful behavior.

### 01  Model compression and execution

cortexSDR is described in the current site materials as an internal C++ toolchain for sparse model archives and on-demand inference. It anchors the brand in concrete infrastructure work. These descriptions are project claims, not a fresh capability audit.

### 02  Alternative computation

The vector-kernel project explores token generation from model embeddings and output weights, alongside routing and snapshot experiments. It demonstrates an experimental approach to execution; its own README documents substantial behavioral limits.

### 03  Representation and understanding

The prompt-interpretation prototype explores explicit relationships, evidence lookup, and programmed response structures. It is a symbolic baseline, which broadens the inquiry into how useful behavior can be represented.

### The brand inference

Selfbyt asks foundational questions, builds working experiments, and studies practical constraints. Efficiency is a research objective that connects the projects, rather than a performance promise already established across all of them.

### What belongs to the future

Original model development is founder-stated ambition. Describe it as a future direction until there is a named project and evidence to present.

## Build. Measure. Learn.

Proposed category: AI infrastructure and research. This is broad enough to include execution tools today and original models later.

### Positioning statement

For engineers and researchers exploring how AI can run more efficiently, Selfbyt builds experimental infrastructure and investigates new representations and execution methods. Its identity comes from working systems, documented experiments, and clear accounts of what those experiments establish.

### Primary audience - proposed

AI engineers, systems developers, and research collaborators who care about memory, inference, model structure, and practical experimentation. Validate this audience as products and users become clearer.

### Infrastructure / present focus

Compression, sparse representations, runtime behavior, model access, and the tools needed to inspect and measure them.

### Experiments / working method

Prototype unusual ideas, compare them with useful baselines, keep the findings, and explain the limits. Research can lead to tools; tools can reveal the next research question.

### Models / future direction

Investigate model architectures and learning methods informed by infrastructure work. The brand should accommodate this without implying that Selfbyt already offers its own model family.

## Ambition, stated plainly.

Use concrete language about what is built, what is being tested, and what remains unknown.

### Short description

Selfbyt builds AI infrastructure and explores new ways to represent and run intelligent systems.

### Expanded description

We experiment with model compression, representations, and execution to understand how AI can work with fewer resources. We turn useful findings into tools, document what we learn, and use that work to inform future model development.

### Homepage headline

Building the foundations for efficient intelligence.

### Homepage supporting copy

We build AI infrastructure and test new ways to represent and run models. Our work connects compression, inference, and experiments in how intelligent systems compute.

### Writing rules

Prefer build, test, measure, inspect, and learn. Name the artifact or question. Give each project a visible status: experiment, internal tool, preview, or released. Write in a calm, confident voice; let the work supply the evidence.

### Claims need context

Publish performance figures with hardware, workload, baseline, and quality measurements. The vector kernel bypasses transformer layers, so its speed alone must not be presented as equivalent full-model inference. Avoid universal model support or preserved-intelligence claims without specific validation.

## Two circles. One clear system.

Keep the existing open circle and filled circle. Refine their geometry and use them consistently across the identity.

Logo proposal: preserve the existing open-left, filled-right arrangement. Equal outer diameters, ring stroke about 12% of diameter, inter-circle gap about 35% of diameter.

### Geometry

Use equal outer diameters; the ring stroke is approximately 12% of that diameter. Leave about 35% of a diameter between the shapes, and half a diameter of clear space around the complete mark. These are prototype proportions to refine at favicon and navigation sizes.

### Meaning - a proposed story

The open circle can suggest a question or possibility; the filled circle can suggest something made concrete. Together they can express experiment and realization. This is a proposed interpretation, not a claim about the original logo meaning.

### Typography and composition

Use a clear sans serif: Inter where available, with system fallbacks. Reserve monospace for measurements, statuses, and labels. Combine strong headlines, generous margins, fine rules, and diagrams that describe actual work.

### Graphic language

Use the circle pair as an organizing device: open questions, tested ideas, connections, and transitions. Keep animation slow and purposeful, with reduced-motion support. Avoid decorative complexity that competes with the projects.

## Ink, paper, and a signal.

Recommended starting direction: warm paper and deep ink, with cobalt as a focused accent. Test this palette on both project pages and technical documents.

Recommended palette: Ink #131820; Paper #F4F2EC; Cobalt #365CF5; Slate #555F6B.

### Roles

Paper is the main reading surface; ink carries headlines, body text, and occasional dark sections. Cobalt marks primary actions, selected states, and the logo. Slate supports secondary text. Start near 75% neutral surfaces, 20% ink, and 5% accent; this is a compositional guide.

### Accessible use

Use white text on cobalt buttons and ink text on paper. Use paper-colored text on ink. Cobalt is strongest as a fill on dark backgrounds; avoid small cobalt text on ink. Always pair status colors with labels or shapes.

Alternative accent: Oxide #B6472C. Warm, material, and workshop-like.

Alternative accent: Acid #D8F36A. Experimental, with continuity from the current lime.

## Let the work lead.

The redesign should make AI infrastructure visible immediately, then give visitors real projects and experiments to explore.

### 01  A clear opening

Lead with the infrastructure headline, one short explanation, and the circle mark at a meaningful size. Primary action: Explore our work. Secondary action: About Selfbyt.

### 02  Work before the archive

Place a featured project immediately after the hero, beginning with cortexSDR and its actual status. Describe the problem, the artifact, and what a visitor can inspect or request. Link to a project detail page when one exists.

### 03  A place for experiments

Present research questions with concise findings and explicit limits. Separate experiments from available products. Keep writing and papers as supporting evidence, populated by actual content.

### 04  Room for models

Mention model development in the company direction. Introduce a Models section only when there is concrete work to show. Use simple navigation: Work, Research, About, Contact.

### 05  Brand consistency

Update the homepage, About copy, footer, metadata, social previews, favicon, and logo together. Remove the stale neuroscience/HCI positioning from root metadata. Retain CMS content and functional contact paths.

### Next design experiment

Apply cobalt, oxide, and acid to the same homepage layout and compare them side by side. Judge clarity, logo recognition, technical credibility, and comfort when reading long project pages. This draft recommends cobalt but does not lock the identity.
