# Selfbyt brand and assets

Selfbyt builds AI infrastructure and experiments with how models are represented and executed. Model development is a future direction. The brand should communicate curiosity, precision, and ambition through concrete work and clear language.

## Current identity

- Logo: an open circle on the left and a filled circle on the right, with equal outer diameters.
- Cobalt: `#365CF5`
- Paper: `#F4F2EC`
- Ink: `#131820`
- Slate: `#555F6B`
- Website headline: “Intelligence. Built from the foundations.”
- Description: “AI infrastructure and experimental research.”

Use generous space, simple typography, fine rules, and geometric diagrams. Avoid em dashes in titles and subtitles. Do not use “Independent thinking. Intelligent systems.” or position cortexSDR as the final product. The company’s direction is broader than sparse models.

## Social assets

[Preview](social/preview.png) · [Usage guide](social/README.md) · [Download kit](selfbyt-social-kit.zip)

`social/` contains upload-ready PNGs and editable SVGs for profile icons, covers, brand graphics, and post templates. Start with `profile-cobalt-400.png`. Replace sample text in templates before publishing.

Regenerate the assets with:

```sh
npm run assets:social
```

The generator is `scripts/generate-social-assets.mjs`. After editing or regenerating, review the PNGs and refresh the ZIP from the contents of `brand/social/`.
